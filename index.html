Gossip Pod

Group voice chat without the internet

Talk and text through your headphones in noisy places. Everyone joins the same hotspot or Wi-Fi, scans a QR code, and talks.

How it works

  1. Everyone connects to the same phone hotspot or Wi-Fi network.
  2. One person taps Create room and shows the QR code.
  3. Everyone else taps Scan QR to join.
  4. Put on your headphones, then talk or chat.

Voice and messages travel directly between the phones in the room. There are no accounts and no servers.
